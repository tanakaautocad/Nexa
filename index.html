About Nexa

Independent service.
Professional standards.

Nexa Accounting is an independent freelance bookkeeping and financial reporting service focused on helping businesses keep their financial information organised, accurate and useful.

As a freelance service, Nexa works directly with clients rather than operating from a traditional accounting office. This allows for a more personal, flexible and focused approach to each client's bookkeeping and reporting needs.
